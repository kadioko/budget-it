## Full Description

```text
Budget It helps you understand where your money is going and stay on track with simple, practical budgeting tools.

Track income and expenses, organize spending by category, set monthly and daily targets, and monitor your progress throughout each budget cycle. Budget It also supports envelopes and accounts, savings goals, recurring transactions, spending insights, and alerts that help you spot overspending early.

Key features:
- Track income and expenses
- Set daily and monthly budget targets
- Use custom budget-cycle start days
- Create category spending limits
- Manage envelopes and account balances
- Move money between envelopes
- Track savings goals with target dates
- Add recurring transactions
- View analytics and spending trends
- Get overspend warnings, weekly summaries, and recurring reminders
- Use English or Swahili language settings
- Access quick in-app guides when you need help

Budget It is designed for everyday personal budgeting. It keeps the experience simple while still giving you useful insights into your spending habits.
```
