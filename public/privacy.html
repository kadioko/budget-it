Budget It

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 6, 2026

Budget It helps users track spending, manage budgets, set savings goals, and review financial activity. This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is protected.

Information We Collect

When you use Budget It, we may collect and store:

  • Account information such as email address and authentication details.
  • Budget information such as spending targets, currency, category limits, envelopes, and savings goals.
  • Transaction information entered by you, including amounts, categories, notes, dates, merchants, tags, and recurring transaction settings.
  • Notification preferences and generated budget alerts.
  • Basic technical information needed to operate the app, such as device/browser information and session data.

How We Use Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide budgeting, transaction tracking, analytics, alerts, and account features.
  • Sync your data across devices when you sign in.
  • Send or display reminders, overspend warnings, weekly summaries, and recurring transaction alerts when enabled.
  • Improve app reliability, security, and user experience.

Data Storage and Security

Budget It uses Supabase for authentication, database storage, and backend functions. We use access controls and row-level security so users can only access their own data. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only with service providers needed to operate the app, when required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of users and the app.

Your Choices

You can update your budget data, notification preferences, and account settings inside the app. You may request account or data deletion by contacting support.

Children's Privacy

Budget It is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new updated date.
